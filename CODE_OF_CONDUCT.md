# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment includes:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct.html).
